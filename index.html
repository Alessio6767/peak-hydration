Your questions,
answered.

Is Peak Hydration safe to drink every day? +

Absolutely. It's clean, gently alkaline (pH 7.6) daily drinking water with balanced minerals naturally found in the body. It's built to be your everyday default, not an occasional sports drink.

Will the minerals change the taste? +

No. At 180 TDS the minerals are measurably present but clean on the palate. Light, smooth, and easy to keep drinking all day. No metallic edge, no flatness.

Is it actually alkaline? +

Gently. At pH 7.6 it's non-acidic and easy on digestion, without the hype of high-alkaline products. The full lab composition is printed openly on every label.

Is this just bottled tap water? +

No. Every bottle goes through a 7-stage process. Filtered, stripped completely, then rebuilt mineral by mineral and balanced. Consistent and identical from the first bottle to the ten-thousandth.

Where is it made? +

Bottled in South Africa, in Gauteng and the Western Cape. For orders or stockist enquiries, reach us on WhatsApp or at order@peakhydration.co.za.
